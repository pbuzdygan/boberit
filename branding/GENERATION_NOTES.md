# Notatki generowania brandingu v2

Tryb: wbudowany generator obrazu. Referencje: cztery obrazy dołączone przez użytkownika w rozmowie.

## Role referencji

- obrazy 1–2: konstrukcja znaku — folder lub skrzynka płynnie przechodząca w kratkowany ogon bobra;
- obrazy 3–4: chłodna kolorystyka, cieniowanie, miękkie światło i jakość szerokiego bannera;
- referencje służą określeniu kierunku, nie kopiowaniu gotowych kompozycji i interfejsów.

## Prompt — ikona/logo

Create a new polished Boberit symbol: a container for important things, file folder or archive box that flows seamlessly into the unmistakable broad crosshatched tail of a beaver. Clearly communicate both storage and the beaver connection without showing an animal face or cartoon mascot. The left/top portion is a compact file box with two stored layers; its right/lower wall transitions structurally into a rounded beaver tail with a controlled diamond lattice. Use a premium modern 3D-vector hybrid style, electric blue and cobalt, deep navy shadows, silver-blue facets, cool highlights, restrained rim light and a strong silhouette. No text, initials, abstract ribbons, generic B monogram, shield, checkmark, UI or warm colors.

## Prompt — banner

Create a premium panoramic 3:1 Boberit banner using the folder/archive-box-to-beaver-tail emblem as the main symbol. Use deep navy, cobalt, cool white and silver-blue with controlled cinematic light. Place the emblem on the left and the exact wordmark and slogans to its right: “Boberit”, “One place for everything that matters.” and “Save it now. Find it later.” Add only subtle monochrome suggestions of connected documents and household objects in the far background. No fake UI, random labels, extra words, laptop or phone mockups, warm colors or clutter.
