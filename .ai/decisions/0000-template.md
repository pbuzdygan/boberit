# ADR-0000: Decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: human or approved project role
- Related work: optional link
- Supersedes: none

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that make a durable decision necessary. Link evidence and relevant specifications instead of copying them.

## Decision

State the chosen approach in concrete terms.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Expected benefit.

### Negative or trade-offs

- Cost, limitation, operational burden, or risk accepted by the decision.

## Alternatives considered

- Alternative: reason it was not selected.

## Security, migration, and rollback

- Security effect: none or a concise description.
- Migration: none or the required transition.
- Rollback: how to reverse the decision safely, or why reversal is not practical.

## Verification

State how implementation conformance will be checked.
